Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Effective: September 2026

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Who we are

MailMatic is a free, open-source Google Sheets add-on developed and operated by Rushil Arora (rxshxl100@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy describes what data the add-on accesses and how it is used.

2. What data MailMatic accesses

MailMatic requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • gmail.send — to send emails on your behalf via the Gmail API. MailMatic does not read, modify, or delete any email in your inbox.
  • spreadsheets.currentonly — to read recipient data and write send-status back into the active Google Sheet.
  • userinfo.email — to identify the sending Gmail account.
  • script.external_request — to make HTTPS calls to the Gmail REST API from Apps Script.

3. How we use your data

MailMatic uses the above access exclusively to:

  • Read recipient names and email addresses from the active Sheet tab.
  • Compose and send personalised emails through your Gmail account.
  • Write a send-status value ("sent", "error", etc.) to the Merge status column of the same Sheet.

MailMatic does not:

  • Read, access, store, or share the content of any email in your Gmail inbox, sent mail, drafts, or any other folder.
  • Transmit your data to any third-party server. All execution happens inside Google's Apps Script runtime on your own Google Cloud project.
  • Collect analytics, usage telemetry, or personally identifiable information.

4. Data storage

MailMatic stores a rolling 24-hour send counter in Apps Script's PropertiesService (key: DAILY_TIMESTAMPS). This data is stored entirely within your own Google account and is never transmitted outside it. No data is stored on external servers.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. The Google API data accessed by MailMatic is used solely to provide the mail-merge sending feature and for no other purpose.

6. Security

MailMatic runs entirely within Google's infrastructure (Apps Script + Gmail API). Authentication is handled by Google OAuth 2.0. We do not handle or store OAuth tokens ourselves; tokens are managed by Google's Apps Script runtime.

7. Your rights

You can revoke MailMatic's access at any time via your Google Account permissions page. Revoking access immediately stops the add-on from sending emails or reading your Sheet.

8. Children's privacy

MailMatic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when changes were last made. Continued use of the add-on after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please email rxshxl100@gmail.com.
